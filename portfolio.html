Project summary

The existing website explained almost everything the business did, but gave potential clients little help deciding what mattered to them. I redesigned the information structure, messaging and visual experience to make the offer easier to understand and the next step easier to take.

Role
Website strategy, information architecture, messaging and design
Focus
Communication clarity, perceived value and enquiry journey
Clearer structure More confidence Simpler action

Business situation

SWEDA already had valuable experience, credible services and enough information to answer most potential client questions. The problem wasn't a lack of content.

Over time, the website had accumulated information without a clear sense of what visitors needed first. Services, company details and supporting explanations competed for attention, while the reasons to choose the business—and the route towards making an enquiry—were difficult to find.

The objective

The initial goal was to redesign the website and increase the number of visitors moving towards an enquiry.

But changing the visual design alone would not solve the underlying issue. Before redesigning the pages, I needed to understand where the existing experience was creating hesitation.

So, what was getting in the way?

The central insight

The problem wasn't that visitors couldn't find information. It was that the website made them decide what was important before they were ready to decide anything else.
